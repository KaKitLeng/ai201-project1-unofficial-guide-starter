Neighborhood

Adjacent to the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign and directly west of Midtown, Downtown Champaign is the busy heart of this energetic college town. It is home to the Virginia Theatre, the Orpheum Children's Museum, and the Art Theater. With terrific shops, restaurants, and a vibrant nightlife, Downtown Champaign always has something happening. Local hotspots include the Black Dog Smoke & Ale House and the Blind Pig Brewery.

During the summer (May to October), residents head to the Land Connection Champaign Farmers' Market. College football fans head to Zuppke Field at Memorial Stadium to cheer on the Fighting Illini. Year-round events provide even more to do and include the Street Fest, the Pygmalion Music Festival, Taste of Champaign-Urbana, and the Boneyard Arts Festival.

Great place
I stayed at 51 E John St. for 2 months over this summer, taking over a lease from someone else. I stayed in a 4-bedroom apartment, with two other housemates that I didn’t know. I had the luxury of getting there first, so I didn’t have to share one of the two bathrooms. The kitchen is spacious, with two refrigerators, and tons of counter space. The bathroom is similarly large. the washer and dryer are extremely effective and easily accessible. The hallways in the building are open to the outside, which means that there is excellent ventilation in the entire apartment and smells from cooking do not stick around. The water quality is very good. In terms of the management, they were absolutely wonderful; when I was moving out, they let me have my pick of the items that had been left behind when people left - I got a vacuum :) they were extremely helpful and friendly people at every opportunity! My very very minor nitpicks: the TV in the apartment is a large android TV, which is mounted somewhat too high on the wall. The back of the device is almost completely inaccessible, and it took getting help from friends to take it down to get an HDMI cable into it. This was the essentially the only annoyance I had the entire time. In a review of a furnished apartment, I think it’s useful to list the additional furniture I bought. I got a table and chairs for on the balcony, a small table for next to the bathtub, and a large trash can for in the kitchen. I also had to buy a wifi router, but the internet there was already taken care of- not sure if by the previous tenant or by the building. All in all, this is an excellent property which I would have happily stayed in if I had not had another place lined up!
